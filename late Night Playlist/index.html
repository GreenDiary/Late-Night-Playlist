About Late Night Playlist

What is Late Night Playlist?

A browser-based music player with curated playlists for listening late into the night.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The player is designed for mobile screens, with large play, skip, and seek controls.

Where does the music play from?

The songs play through YouTube's embedded player — nothing is hosted here, all rights remain with the artists and labels.

Is Late Night Playlist free to listen to?

Yes. Late Night Playlist is free to use in your browser and requires no sign-up or app download.
