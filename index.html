About Late Night Playlist

What is Late Night Playlist?

A free browser-based music player for late-night listening, with Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, and lo-fi/instrumental YouTube playlists. No sign-up or app download required.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The player is designed for mobile screens, with large play, skip, and seek controls.

Where does the music play from?

The songs play through YouTube's embedded player — nothing is hosted here, all rights remain with the artists and labels.

Is Late Night Playlist free to listen to?

Yes. Late Night Playlist remains free to use forever in your browser I promise, and requires no sign-up or app download.

CAN I support the project?

Yes, To continue running this website, YOU CAN support it by scanning the UPI QR code or buying me a coffee through the links in the Motivate me button.
